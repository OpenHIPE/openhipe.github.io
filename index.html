Human-Centered Interactive Perception System Evaluation

OpenHIPE is an open-source ecosystem for evaluating XR and robotic systems with people in mind. We build shared tools to capture sensor data, run repeatable experiments, and share results across research communities. Read our vision article.

Capture

Record camera, depth, and 3D scene data from XR devices and robots with the SensorFlex recorder. It works across platforms and keeps every sensor in sync.

Evaluate

Replay recorded sessions in Unity on any platform with the SensorFlex player. Rerun the same experiment, or test your perception system on real-world data.

Share

Save data in SFZ, an open format you can read with Python. Share results and demos online with ARCADE, and gather feedback from real people.
